COMMANDER'S HANDBOOK

FIELD GUIDE

COMMAND THE HERO

Click or tap the ground to move. The hero attacks automatically. Use Sun Nova when surrounded.

RAISE & UPGRADE

Select a tower card, then click a glowing pad or press E nearby. Click a built tower to upgrade, retarget, or sell it.

CONTROL THE RHYTHM

Waves end when the field is clear. During the countdown, press Enter to call the next wave early and earn bonus gold.

BUILD YOUR COMMANDER

Kills and cleared waves earn XP. Every commander level offers a draft of powerful run-long blessings.

KNOW YOUR ENEMIES

Runners rush, bombers explode, brutes soak damage, and bosses can shrug off control. Mix towers to cover their weaknesses.

ESSENTIAL KEYS

1–4 towers · Q upgrade · F speed · Space Nova · Esc pause
